RECIPE: Classic Margherita Pizza
CATEGORY: Italian, Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 20 minutes
COOK TIME: 12 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
- 1 cup warm water (110°F)
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 cup San Marzano tomato sauce
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
- Fresh basil leaves
- 2 tbsp olive oil (for drizzling)
- Salt and pepper to taste

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water; let sit 5 minutes until foamy.
2. Mix flour and salt in a large bowl. Add yeast mixture and olive oil. Knead for 8 minutes until smooth.
3. Cover dough and let rise in a warm place for 1 hour until doubled.
4. Preheat oven to 500°F (260°C) with a pizza stone if available.
5. Punch down dough and roll out into a 12-inch circle on a floured surface.
6. Transfer to a baking sheet or pizza stone. Spread tomato sauce evenly.
7. Arrange mozzarella slices on top. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Bake for 10-12 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
9. Remove from oven, top with fresh basil leaves, and drizzle with olive oil.
10. Slice and serve immediately.

TIPS: For a crispier crust, pre-bake the dough for 5 minutes before adding toppings.
NUTRITION: Calories 320, Protein 14g, Carbs 42g, Fat 11g per serving.

RECIPE: Chicken Tikka Masala
CATEGORY: Indian, Non-Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 30 minutes
COOK TIME: 40 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Medium

INGREDIENTS:
For marinade:
- 1.5 lbs boneless chicken breast, cubed
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt

For sauce:
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Mix marinade ingredients and coat chicken. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour (overnight preferred).
2. Grill or broil marinated chicken at high heat until slightly charred, about 5-7 minutes. Set aside.
3. Melt butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add onions and cook until golden, 10 minutes.
4. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 2 minutes. Add all spices and stir for 1 minute.
5. Pour in crushed tomatoes and simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Blend sauce until smooth (optional). Return to pan and add heavy cream.
7. Add cooked chicken pieces and simmer for 10 more minutes.
8. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with basmati rice or naan.

TIPS: The longer you marinate the chicken, the more flavorful it will be.
NUTRITION: Calories 485, Protein 38g, Carbs 18g, Fat 29g per serving.

RECIPE: Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs
CATEGORY: Breakfast, Vegetarian, Healthy
PREP TIME: 5 minutes
COOK TIME: 10 minutes
SERVINGS: 2
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 4 slices sourdough bread
- 2 ripe avocados
- 4 large eggs
- 2 tbsp white vinegar
- 1 lemon (juice)
- Red pepper flakes
- Everything bagel seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Microgreens or sprouts (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Toast bread slices until golden and crispy.
2. Halve avocados, remove pits, and scoop flesh into a bowl.
3. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper to desired consistency.
4. Fill a medium saucepan with 3 inches of water and bring to a gentle simmer. Add vinegar.
5. Crack each egg into a small cup. Create a gentle swirl in water.
6. Slide egg into the water and poach for 3-4 minutes until whites are set but yolk is runny.
7. Spread mashed avocado generously on toasted bread.
8. Place a poached egg on each slice.
9. Season with red pepper flakes, everything bagel seasoning, salt and pepper.
10. Top with microgreens if using. Serve immediately.

TIPS: Use the freshest eggs possible for the best poached eggs—they hold together better.
NUTRITION: Calories 420, Protein 16g, Carbs 38g, Fat 24g per serving.

RECIPE: Classic Beef Tacos
CATEGORY: Mexican, Non-Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 10 minutes
COOK TIME: 20 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 1 lb ground beef (80/20)
- 8 small corn or flour tortillas
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade)
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 cup diced tomatoes
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup fresh salsa
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Fresh cilantro and lime wedges

HOMEMADE SEASONING:
- 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp oregano, salt

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Brown ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat. Drain excess fat.
2. Add taco seasoning and water. Stir and simmer for 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
3. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave wrapped in a damp paper towel.
4. Assemble tacos: spoon meat into tortillas.
5. Top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, salsa, and avocado.
6. Garnish with cilantro and serve with lime wedges.

TIPS: For crispier shells, lightly fry tortillas in vegetable oil for 30 seconds per side.
NUTRITION: Calories 380, Protein 28g, Carbs 28g, Fat 18g per serving.

RECIPE: Creamy Mushroom Risotto
CATEGORY: Italian, Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 10 minutes
COOK TIME: 35 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Medium

INGREDIENTS:
- 1.5 cups Arborio rice
- 1 lb mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, porcini)
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 5 cups vegetable or chicken broth (kept warm)
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Truffle oil for drizzling (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Warm broth in a separate saucepan over low heat.
2. Heat olive oil and 2 tbsp butter in a wide, heavy pan over medium heat.
3. Sauté mushrooms until golden and all moisture has evaporated, about 8 minutes. Season and set aside.
4. In same pan, sauté onion until soft, 5 minutes. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
5. Add Arborio rice; toast for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
6. Pour in white wine and stir until absorbed.
7. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently and waiting until each addition is absorbed before adding the next.
8. Continue for 20-25 minutes until rice is al dente and creamy.
9. Stir in remaining butter, Parmesan, and cooked mushrooms.
10. Adjust seasoning. Serve immediately topped with thyme and optional truffle oil.

TIPS: Never let the risotto sit dry—always keep some liquid movement going while cooking.
NUTRITION: Calories 445, Protein 12g, Carbs 65g, Fat 16g per serving.

RECIPE: Chocolate Lava Cake
CATEGORY: Dessert, Baking
PREP TIME: 15 minutes
COOK TIME: 12 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Medium

INGREDIENTS:
- 4 oz dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 6 tbsp all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- Cocoa powder for dusting
- Ice cream or whipped cream for serving

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Butter four 6-oz ramekins and dust with cocoa powder.
2. Melt chocolate and butter in a microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring between, until smooth.
3. Whisk in powdered sugar until combined.
4. Add eggs and egg yolks one at a time, whisking well after each addition.
5. Mix in vanilla extract and pinch of salt.
6. Fold in flour until just combined—do not overmix.
7. Divide batter evenly among prepared ramekins.
8. Bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are firm but center is still soft and jiggly.
9. Let cool in ramekins for 1 minute, then carefully invert onto plates.
10. Serve immediately with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.

TIPS: Ramekins can be prepared and refrigerated up to 24 hours ahead—just add 1-2 minutes to bake time.
NUTRITION: Calories 520, Protein 8g, Carbs 58g, Fat 30g per serving.

RECIPE: Greek Salad
CATEGORY: Greek, Vegetarian, Healthy, Salad
PREP TIME: 15 minutes
COOK TIME: 0 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 4 large ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 large cucumber, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese block
- 2 tbsp dried oregano

For dressing:
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Combine tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, and red onion in a large bowl.
2. Add Kalamata olives to the bowl.
3. Whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, oregano, salt and pepper.
4. Pour dressing over vegetables and toss gently.
5. Place feta cheese block on top (traditionally not crumbled).
6. Sprinkle generously with dried oregano.
7. Let sit for 5-10 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.
8. Serve with crusty bread.

TIPS: Use the best quality olive oil and feta you can find—it makes a huge difference.
NUTRITION: Calories 245, Protein 8g, Carbs 12g, Fat 19g per serving.

RECIPE: Pad Thai
CATEGORY: Thai, Non-Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 20 minutes
COOK TIME: 15 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Medium

INGREDIENTS:
- 8 oz flat rice noodles
- 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined (or chicken/tofu)
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 shallots, minced
- Lime wedges for serving

Pad Thai Sauce:
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp chili sauce

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Soak rice noodles in room temperature water for 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
2. Mix all sauce ingredients together and set aside.
3. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
4. Add shrimp/protein and cook until done, about 3-4 minutes. Push to the side.
5. Add garlic and shallots; stir-fry for 30 seconds.
6. Add drained noodles and pour sauce over. Toss to combine.
7. Push everything to the side and scramble eggs in the empty space.
8. Mix eggs into the noodle mixture.
9. Add bean sprouts and half the green onions. Toss for 1 minute.
10. Plate and top with peanuts, remaining green onions, and lime wedges.

TIPS: Cook on very high heat for the authentic wok-fried flavor (wok hei).
NUTRITION: Calories 465, Protein 26g, Carbs 52g, Fat 16g per serving.

RECIPE: Banana Smoothie Bowl
CATEGORY: Breakfast, Vegan, Healthy
PREP TIME: 10 minutes
COOK TIME: 0 minutes
SERVINGS: 2
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
Base:
- 3 frozen bananas
- 1/2 cup frozen mango chunks
- 1/4 cup coconut milk (or almond milk)
- 1 tbsp almond butter

Toppings:
- Fresh banana slices
- Granola
- Chia seeds
- Fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries)
- Coconut flakes
- Honey or maple syrup drizzle
- Hemp seeds

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Add frozen bananas, frozen mango, coconut milk, and almond butter to a high-speed blender.
2. Blend until thick and creamy, scraping down the sides as needed.
3. The consistency should be thicker than a drinkable smoothie—add less liquid for a thicker base.
4. Pour into two bowls.
5. Arrange toppings decoratively: granola, banana slices, berries, chia seeds, coconut flakes.
6. Drizzle with honey or maple syrup.
7. Sprinkle with hemp seeds.
8. Serve immediately before it melts.

TIPS: Keep everything frozen! The colder your fruits, the thicker your bowl. Never add too much liquid.
NUTRITION: Calories 310, Protein 6g, Carbs 62g, Fat 8g per serving.

RECIPE: Beef Stir Fry with Vegetables
CATEGORY: Asian, Non-Vegetarian, Quick
PREP TIME: 15 minutes
COOK TIME: 12 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 1 lb flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 cup snap peas
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Stir Fry Sauce:
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/4 cup beef broth

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Slice beef thinly. Marinate in 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tsp cornstarch for 10 minutes.
2. Mix all sauce ingredients together in a bowl.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in wok over very high heat until smoking.
4. Stir-fry beef in batches for 2-3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.
5. Add remaining oil to wok. Stir-fry carrots and broccoli for 3 minutes.
6. Add bell pepper and snap peas; stir-fry 2 more minutes.
7. Add garlic and ginger; stir-fry 30 seconds until fragrant.
8. Return beef to wok. Pour sauce over everything.
9. Toss until sauce coats everything evenly and thickens, about 1-2 minutes.
10. Serve over steamed rice or noodles.

TIPS: Slice beef against the grain and partially freeze it beforehand for thinner, more tender slices.
NUTRITION: Calories 340, Protein 32g, Carbs 18g, Fat 16g per serving.

RECIPE: Lemon Garlic Butter Salmon
CATEGORY: Seafood, Healthy, Quick
PREP TIME: 5 minutes
COOK TIME: 15 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon (zest and juice)
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Lemon slices for serving

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Pat salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Place salmon skin-side up and cook for 4 minutes without moving.
4. Flip salmon. Preheat broiler to high.
5. Add butter and garlic to pan. Baste salmon with melted garlic butter as it cooks.
6. Transfer pan to broiler for 3-4 minutes until salmon is cooked through and lightly caramelized.
7. Remove from broiler. Squeeze lemon juice over fillets.
8. Sprinkle with lemon zest and fresh parsley.
9. Serve immediately with lemon slices and your choice of sides.

TIPS: Don't overcook salmon—it should still be slightly translucent in the center and flake easily.
NUTRITION: Calories 385, Protein 42g, Carbs 2g, Fat 23g per serving.

RECIPE: Vegetable Curry
CATEGORY: Indian, Vegan, Vegetarian
PREP TIME: 15 minutes
COOK TIME: 30 minutes
SERVINGS: 4
DIFFICULTY: Easy

INGREDIENTS:
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp coriander
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup cauliflower florets
- 1 cup chickpeas (canned, drained)
- 1 cup spinach
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Basmati rice for serving

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until golden, 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook 2 minutes. Add all spices and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in coconut milk and diced tomatoes. Stir to combine.
4. Add potatoes and cauliflower. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 15 minutes.
5. Add chickpeas and cook another 5 minutes until potatoes are tender.
6. Stir in spinach until wilted, about 2 minutes.
7. Adjust salt and seasoning to taste.
8. Serve over basmati rice and garnish with fresh cilantro.

TIPS: For a richer flavor, add a spoonful of tomato paste with the spices.
NUTRITION: Calories 310, Protein 9g, Carbs 42g, Fat 14g per serving.
